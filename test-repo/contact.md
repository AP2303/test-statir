## Whistleblower channel

Concerns about misconduct, conflict of interest, or breaches of ADB's Code of Conduct can be reported confidentially to [ethics@adb.al](mailto:ethics@adb.al) or via our independent integrity helpline (+355 4 220 0099). Reports can be submitted anonymously.
