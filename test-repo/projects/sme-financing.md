# SME Financing

Small and medium enterprises represent **over 99% of Albanian businesses** and roughly **70% of private-sector employment**. They are also the segment most underserved by long-term, productive-investment lending. ADB's SME programme addresses this gap.

## How it works

We do not lend directly to individual SMEs. Instead, we extend **wholesale credit lines** to qualified commercial banks and microfinance institutions, which on-lend the funds as sub-loans to SMEs under terms agreed with ADB.

```
   ADB ──[wholesale facility]──▶ Partner Bank ──[sub-loan]──▶ SME Borrower
                                       │
                                       └─[reporting & monitoring]─▶ ADB
```

## Eligibility — partner financial institutions

To qualify as a partner, a bank or MFI must:

- Hold a valid licence from the Bank of Albania
- Maintain capital adequacy above the regulatory minimum + 2 pp buffer
- Have a non-performing-loan ratio below 8%
- Pass ADB's environmental, social, and governance review
- Demonstrate a viable SME lending strategy

## Eligibility — sub-loan recipients

Sub-loans must go to SMEs that:

- Are registered in Albania
- Employ fewer than 250 staff
- Have annual turnover below €50 M (or balance-sheet total below €43 M)
- Operate in a sector outside ADB's Exclusion List
- Use proceeds for **productive investment** — equipment, premises, working capital tied to growth, energy efficiency, R&D, or export development

## Sub-loan parameters

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Sub-loan size | €5 K — €1 M |
| Tenor | 1 — 7 years |
| Grace period | Up to 12 months |
| Currency | EUR or ALL |
| Collateral | Per partner-bank policy; minimum 70% LTV recommended |

## Active partner banks

ADB currently has signed wholesale facilities with five partner banks. The list is updated quarterly on the [News](../news/2026-04-launch.html) page.

## Impact reporting

Partner banks report quarterly to ADB with:

- Number and total volume of sub-loans disbursed
- Sectoral and geographic breakdown
- Jobs supported
- Environmental and gender indicators (where applicable)

ADB consolidates this data and publishes annual impact figures in the **Development Impact Report**.

## Becoming a partner bank

Interested banks and MFIs should contact [sme@adb.al](mailto:sme@adb.al) for the qualification questionnaire and an introductory meeting.
