# Infrastructure Projects

A snapshot of the infrastructure operations ADB is financing across Albania. Figures reflect ADB's commitment; total project costs are typically larger thanks to co-financing.

## Active operations

### Tirana — Durrës Rail Modernisation
**ADB commitment:** €120 M · **Total project:** €420 M · **Co-financiers:** EIB, EBRD

Electrification, signalling renewal, and station upgrades on Albania's busiest passenger corridor. ADB acts as the local financing partner, providing ALL-denominated tranches that hedge the sponsor's currency exposure.

- Status: **Construction (45% physical progress)**
- Expected completion: Q4 2027

### Coastal Water Resilience Programme
**ADB commitment:** €55 M · **Total project:** €110 M · **Co-financiers:** KfW, CEB

Reservoir capacity, leak reduction, and coastal-aquifer protection across six municipalities (Vlorë, Sarandë, Himarë, Lezhë, Shkodër, Durrës).

- Status: **Procurement underway**
- Expected first disbursement: Q3 2026

### National Backbone Fibre Extension
**ADB commitment:** €40 M · **Total project:** €95 M · **Co-financiers:** EBRD, private operator

A public-private partnership to extend high-capacity fibre to underserved municipalities, financed through a viability-gap mechanism.

- Status: **Disbursing**
- Coverage target: 220,000 additional households by 2028

### Solar IPP Tranche II
**ADB commitment:** €35 M · **Total project:** €70 M · **Co-financiers:** EIB, private sponsors

Three utility-scale photovoltaic plants totalling 180 MWp, contributing to Albania's 2030 renewable-energy target.

- Status: **Operational** (commissioned March 2026)
- Annual generation: ~310 GWh

## Pipeline (2026 — 2027)
