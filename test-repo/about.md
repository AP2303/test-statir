# About the Bank

The Albanian Development Bank (ADB) is the country's specialised institution for long-term development financing. We mobilise public and private capital toward investments that strengthen Albania's productive capacity, public infrastructure, and economic resilience.

## Our mission

> Financing sustainable growth, modern infrastructure, and competitive enterprises across Albania and the Western Balkans.

We deliver this mission through three complementary roles:

1. **Long-term lender** — concessional and market-rate financing to public and private projects
2. **Patient investor** — equity participation in strategic enterprises and venture funds
3. **Trusted partner** — co-financing platform for international financial institutions

## Mandate

ADB operates under the *Law on the Albanian Development Bank* (Law No. 142/2024). Our mandate covers:

- Infrastructure financing (transport, energy, water, digital)
- Productive sector lending (manufacturing, agribusiness, tourism)
- SME and innovation financing through approved intermediaries
- Climate and green-transition investments
- Project preparation and technical assistance

We do not compete with commercial banks — we complement them. Where the market alone cannot provide long-tenor or risk-bearing capital, ADB steps in.

## A brief history

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **2024** | Founding act passed by the Assembly of Albania |
| **2025** | Initial paid-in capital of €350 M; first lending operations approved |
| **2025** | Memoranda of understanding signed with EIB, EBRD, KfW, and IFC |
| **2026** | Launch of the Green Infrastructure Window (€500 M commitment ceiling) |
| **2026** | Aa3 rating affirmed by Moody's |

## Capital structure

ADB is wholly owned by the Republic of Albania. Subscribed capital stands at **€1.2 billion** with paid-in capital of **€450 million** as of Q1 2026. Capital adequacy is maintained well above the regulatory minimum, with a Tier 1 ratio of 22.4%.

## How we operate

We are headquartered in Tirana with regional offices in Durrës, Vlorë, and Shkodër. Project origination is led by sectoral teams (Infrastructure, Energy, SME & Innovation, Climate). Approval thresholds are tiered — operations above €25 M require Board sign-off; smaller transactions are delegated to the Management Committee.
