# ADB Launches the Green Infrastructure Window

**Tirana, 22 April 2026** — The Albanian Development Bank today announced the launch of its **Green Infrastructure Window**, a €500 million concessional financing facility dedicated to climate-aligned infrastructure investments across Albania.

The Window will provide long-tenor, below-market loans to public and private projects that meet ADB's green-eligibility criteria, in line with the **EU Taxonomy** and the **Paris Agreement** alignment standards adopted by major multilateral development banks.

## Eligible activities

The Window targets four investment areas:

1. **Renewable energy** — solar, wind, small hydro, energy storage
2. **Energy efficiency** — building retrofits, district heating, industrial efficiency
3. **Sustainable transport** — public transit electrification, rail, active mobility
4. **Climate adaptation** — water, flood resilience, coastal protection

All projects financed under the Window must demonstrate clear greenhouse-gas emission reductions or measurable adaptation benefits, verified by an independent technical reviewer.

## Pricing and terms

| Parameter | Standard term | Window term |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Tenor | up to 15 yrs | **up to 25 yrs** |
| Pricing | market-rate | **up to 200 bps below market** |
| Grace period | up to 3 yrs | **up to 5 yrs** |
| Maximum exposure | €50 M | **€80 M** |

## Funding sources

The Window is financed by a blend of:

- ADB's own resources (€200 M)
- A €200 M concessional credit line from KfW
- A €100 M grant-funded interest-rate subsidy from the Climate Investment Funds, channelled via the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development

## Target portfolio

ADB expects to commit the full €500 M over **five years (2026 — 2030)**, with an indicative target of:

- **40 — 50 operations**
- **5 — 7 million tonnes** of cumulative CO₂ emissions avoided
- **At least 30%** of volume directed to projects benefiting underserved municipalities

## Quote

> "The Green Infrastructure Window is the most ambitious climate-finance initiative ever launched in Albania. It puts our institution at the heart of the country's green transition and demonstrates that development banking and climate action go hand in hand."
>
> — *Chief Executive Officer, Albanian Development Bank*

## How to apply

Project sponsors interested in financing under the Window should submit an initial enquiry to [green@adb.al](mailto:green@adb.al). Detailed eligibility guidelines and the application form are available on request.
