# Q1 2026 Quarterly Report

**Tirana, 31 March 2026** — The Albanian Development Bank closed the first quarter of 2026 with continued growth in lending volumes, a strengthened balance sheet, and the affirmation of its **Aa3** rating by Moody's.

## Headline figures

| Indicator | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 | Δ |
|-----------|---------|---------|---|
| New approvals | €185 M | €128 M | +44.5% |
| Disbursements | €142 M | €94 M | +51.1% |
| Loan portfolio (gross) | €1.84 B | €1.42 B | +29.6% |
| Equity portfolio (cost) | €112 M | €78 M | +43.6% |
| Non-performing loan ratio | 1.6% | 1.9% | -30 bps |
| Capital adequacy (Tier 1) | 22.4% | 21.8% | +60 bps |
| Net profit | €11.2 M | €7.8 M | +43.6% |

## Lending activity

Eight operations totalling **€185 million** were approved in the quarter, including:

- **Coastal Water Resilience Programme** — €55 M (water sector)
- **National Backbone Fibre Extension** — €40 M (digital)
- **Solar IPP Tranche II** — €35 M (energy)
- **SME Wholesale Facility — Banka Kombëtare Tregtare** — €30 M (SME on-lending)
- Four smaller productive-sector operations totalling €25 M

## Funding

ADB completed its inaugural **€250 million 7-year benchmark bond** in February, priced at MS+85 bps and oversubscribed 2.4 times. The transaction marks the Bank's establishment as a regular issuer in international capital markets.

## Rating action

On 14 March 2026, Moody's affirmed ADB's long-term issuer rating at **Aa3** with a **stable** outlook. The agency cited the Bank's:

- Strong capital position and conservative leverage
- High-quality loan portfolio with low NPL ratio
- Implicit support from the Republic of Albania
- Robust governance and risk-management framework

## Outlook

The Board reaffirmed the **2026 lending target of €750 million** and noted the imminent launch of the [Green Infrastructure Window](2026-04-launch.html), which is expected to drive incremental commitments of €100 — €150 million during the remainder of the year.
