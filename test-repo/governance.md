# Governance

ADB is governed by a two-tier structure — a **Supervisory Board** that sets strategy and oversees risk, and a **Management Committee** that runs day-to-day operations. Independent auditors and a Risk Committee report directly to the Supervisory Board.

## Supervisory Board

The Supervisory Board is composed of seven members, including three independent directors. It approves the annual budget, the strategic plan, large operations, and risk-appetite limits.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| **Chair** | Sets the agenda; represents the Bank externally |
| **Vice-Chair** | Stands in for the Chair; chairs the Risk Committee |
| **Independent Directors (3)** | Bring sector expertise; chair specialised committees |
| **State Representatives (2)** | Appointed by the Ministry of Finance and Economy |

Board meetings are held quarterly with extraordinary sessions called as needed.

## Management Committee

The Management Committee is led by the Chief Executive Officer and includes the heads of the Bank's main functions:

- **CEO** — overall leadership and external representation
- **Chief Operating Officer** — operations, IT, HR
- **Chief Risk Officer** — credit, market, and operational risk
- **Chief Investment Officer** — portfolio management, equity investments
- **Chief Financial Officer** — treasury, funding, financial reporting
- **General Counsel** — legal, compliance, governance

## Committees

The Bank operates four standing committees:

1. **Risk Committee** — reviews credit decisions above €5 M, monitors portfolio health
2. **Audit Committee** — oversees internal and external audit functions
3. **Investment Committee** — approves equity transactions and fund commitments
4. **Remuneration & Nomination Committee** — sets executive compensation policy

## External oversight

ADB is supervised by the **Bank of Albania** under the prudential framework applicable to public financial institutions. Annual financial statements are audited by an independent firm (currently Ernst & Young Albania) and published within four months of year-end.

## Code of conduct

All Board members, executives, and staff are bound by ADB's Code of Conduct, which addresses conflicts of interest, anti-corruption, gift policies, and whistleblower protections. Suspected violations can be reported confidentially via [ethics@adb.al](mailto:ethics@adb.al).
