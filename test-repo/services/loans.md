# Lending Programs

ADB's core activity is long-term lending for projects that contribute to Albania's economic development. We offer competitive rates, extended tenors, and grace periods that match project cash flows — terms commercial banks rarely provide.

## Eligible borrowers

- **Public sector** — central government agencies, municipalities, public utilities
- **Private sector** — companies registered in Albania with sound financial standing
- **Financial intermediaries** — commercial banks and microfinance institutions on-lending to SMEs

## Product overview

### Infrastructure Loans
Financing for transport, water, energy, and digital infrastructure.

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Loan size | €5 M — €100 M |
| Tenor | Up to 25 years |
| Grace period | Up to 5 years |
| Currency | EUR, USD, ALL |

### Energy & Climate Loans
Concessional financing for renewable energy, energy efficiency, and climate-adaptation projects, partly funded by the Green Infrastructure Window.

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Loan size | €1 M — €50 M |
| Tenor | Up to 20 years |
| Pricing | Up to 200 bps below market |
| Currency | EUR primarily |

### Productive Sector Loans
Term loans for manufacturing, agribusiness, tourism, and export-oriented industries.

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Loan size | €500 K — €25 M |
| Tenor | 3 — 12 years |
| Grace period | Up to 2 years |
| Currency | EUR, ALL |

### SME On-Lending
Wholesale credit lines extended to commercial banks and MFIs for sub-loans to small and medium enterprises.

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Wholesale facility size | €10 M — €75 M |
| Sub-loan size | Up to €1 M |
| Tenor | Up to 7 years |
| Currency | EUR, ALL |

## Application process

1. **Initial enquiry** — submit a project profile via [info@adb.al](mailto:info@adb.al)
2. **Eligibility screening** — ADB reviews mandate fit within 10 business days
3. **Due diligence** — financial, technical, environmental, and legal review
4. **Credit decision** — Management Committee or Board approval
5. **Loan agreement** — documentation and signing
6. **Disbursement** — against agreed milestones and disbursement conditions

Typical end-to-end timeline: **3 — 6 months** for infrastructure loans, **6 — 10 weeks** for productive-sector loans.

## Pricing principles

- Pricing reflects credit risk, tenor, and currency.
- Concessional pricing is reserved for projects meeting strict eligibility criteria (climate, social impact, regional development).
- All-in cost is disclosed transparently in the term sheet — no hidden fees.
