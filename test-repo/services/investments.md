# Investments

Beyond lending, ADB takes **minority equity positions** in companies and funds that drive economic transformation. We are a patient investor — typical holding periods are 5 — 10 years — and our capital is paired with active board engagement and access to our network.

## Investment thesis

We invest where:

- The company is a **market leader or credible challenger** in its sector
- There is a clear path to **scale, export, or modernisation**
- Our equity catalyses **additional private investment** that wouldn't otherwise materialise
- The transaction passes ADB's **environmental, social, and governance (ESG)** screen

We do **not** invest in real-estate speculation, commodity trading, or sectors excluded by our Exclusion List (gambling, tobacco, fossil-fuel exploration, weapons).

## How we invest

### Direct equity
- Ticket size: **€2 M — €30 M**
- Stake: typically 10 — 30%, always minority
- Instruments: ordinary shares, preferred shares, convertible loans
- Exit: trade sale, secondary buyout, IPO, or buyback

### Fund commitments
- We commit as a Limited Partner to private equity, venture, and infrastructure funds operating in or focused on Albania and the Western Balkans
- Commitment range: **€5 M — €25 M** per fund
- Preference for funds with strong ESG and impact frameworks

### Co-investment
- ADB co-invests alongside its fund managers and other strategic partners
- Co-investment ticket sizes typically **€1 M — €10 M**

## Engagement model

ADB's investment professionals serve on portfolio-company boards or as observers. We bring not only capital but also access to international financial institutions, strategic partners, and industry expertise. We hold ourselves and our investees to high governance standards — transparent reporting, regular audits, and adherence to ESG commitments are non-negotiable.
