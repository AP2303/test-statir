# Partnerships

ADB is built to be a **bridge** — between Albanian project sponsors and the international financial system, and between domestic public investment priorities and global capital markets. We co-finance, syndicate, and structure operations alongside the institutions below.

## Strategic partners

| Partner | Type of cooperation |
|---------|---------------------|
| **European Investment Bank (EIB)** | Co-financing of infrastructure operations; technical assistance facility |
| **European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD)** | Joint SME lending platform; equity co-investment |
| **KfW Development Bank** | Climate and renewable-energy lending program |
| **International Finance Corporation (IFC)** | Private-sector advisory; trade-finance lines |
| **Council of Europe Development Bank (CEB)** | Social-infrastructure financing |
| **World Bank Group** | Project preparation and capacity building |

## How co-financing works

In a typical co-financing structure, ADB and a partner each commit a portion of the loan, sharing security and risk pari-passu. This lets borrowers access **larger ticket sizes**, **longer tenors**, and **standardised documentation** under international best practice.

## Technical assistance

Several of our partnerships include a **technical-assistance (TA) component** that funds:

- Feasibility studies and project preparation
- Environmental and social impact assessments
- Procurement support
- Capacity building for project sponsors and municipalities

In 2025, ADB managed **€18 M** of TA grants on behalf of partner institutions.

## Becoming a partner

We welcome cooperation with development banks, commercial banks, multilateral institutions, and impact investors. To explore a partnership, contact our International Cooperation team at [partnerships@adb.al](mailto:partnerships@adb.al).
